HOW TO MAKE A FILM

🐾 MAKE — build your characters. Pick a skeleton (a person, a dog, a bird, a snake, a bug, a fish, a dino or a blob), then their fur, colours, patterns, ears, tail, horns and wings.

🌍 STAGE — choose where it happens and what the lighting is like. Tap the ground to stand your character somewhere. Drag to spin the view around.

🎬 MOVES — this is the timeline. Drag the ▶ marker to a time, then add a move (walk, dance, jump, wave, sleep…). Add a spot in two different places at two different times and your character will walk from one to the other.

📷 SHOTS — point the camera how you like it, then press ADD SHOT. Add more shots at different times and the camera will glide — or set it to CUT to snap straight there.

🍿 FILM — watch the whole thing full screen, and record it to keep.
