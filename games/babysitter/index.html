BABY
SITTER

There are babies crawling all over the baby-proofed house — and they all want something!

✳
The tiny X in the middle of the screen is where your hands are.
✋
Press the hand to pick up whatever is in the X. You can hold 2 things.
✊
Press the fist to put something down.
😢
Pick up a crying baby, fetch the thing it wants, and it goes happy — that's a heart.
👶
5 hearts = a new baby. Get 10 babies and make them all happy at once to win!

Keyboard: arrows / WASD to move  ·  SPACE or E = pick up  ·  Q or F = put down

YOU WIN!

All 10 babies are happy at the same time. Nobody is crying. The house is still standing.
You are the best babysitter.
